## Processing applied

- Trimmed to 81s (film length) with a 1.5s fade-in and 2s fade-out.
- Volume reduced to 50% of source, then sidechain-compressed keyed by the real VO track (`vo-final.mp3`) so the bed ducks under actual speech and recovers in real pauses, not a hand-timed envelope.
